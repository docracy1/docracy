# Warm DM / email scripts

Send ~20/week. Personalize the first line. Always use **https://docracy.io/go/dm** (short).

## Who to message

- Freelancers (design, eng, consulting) who send client NDAs
- Agency ops / producers who onboard contractors
- People ops at 5–30 person companies
- Anyone publicly complaining about DocuSign / HelloSign pricing

Sources: LinkedIn search, X search “DocuSign expensive”, your own signers who reply “what’s this tool?”

## Rules

1. One ask. Don’t list MCP/Zapier/cloud.
2. Never paste a long UTM URL.
3. If they say “not interested,” thank them and stop.
4. Log replies: interested / tried / paid — so you know the channel works.
