# Zero replies — what it means + what to do

Batches 1 + 2 + partners ≈ **40+ cold emails**, **0 replies**.

That is **bad luck or deliverability**, not proof the product is wrong. Cold founder email to shared inboxes (`hello@`, `info@`, `contact@`) often lands at **0–3%** reply. But we should change the play — don’t send batch 3 with the same “curious what you use?” template.

---

## 1. Deliverability — done

Founder mail reaches inboxes (confirmed). **Spam is not the explanation.** Treat 0 replies as: weak ask, cold shared inboxes, or ICP not in buying mode — then change channel/message, don’t send more of the same.

---

## 2. One soft follow-up only (not a new blast)

**When:** 5–7 days after the original (batch 1 partners ≈ now; batch 2 ≈ early next week).  
**Who:** people who did **not** bounce; skip anyone who said no.  
**Max:** one follow-up. Then stop that lead.

**Subject:** (different from first mail)

`30-second NDA — no account`

```
Hey {{name}} —

Last note in case the earlier one got buried.

If you ever need a lightweight NDA / client agreement signed: free for 2 people, no signup —
https://docracy.io/try

No deck, no call. Ignore if not relevant.

Odo
Founder, Docracy
https://docracy.io
```

**Change vs batch 1/2:** gift the product (`/try`), don’t ask “what do you use?” (that question is easy to ignore).

---

## 3. Stop doing / start doing

| Stop | Start |
|------|--------|
| Same curiosity email to more strangers | Follow-up above once, then pause cold email 1–2 weeks |
| Measuring success by “emails sent” | Measure Admin → Growth: `/try` visits, `document_sent`, opt-ins |
| Hoping shared inboxes reply | Prefer **named** people (`claudia@`, `dani@`) over `info@` / `hello@` next time |
| More feature posts | Post **mobile video** once; reply to any inbound |

---

## 4. Where customers can still come from (no replies needed)

1. **Product loop** — anyone who already sent/signed + opt-in drip → `/try` / login (already live)
2. **SEO** — HelloSign / PandaDoc / Adobe / DocuSign pages compounding (slow)
3. **Mobile video** — LinkedIn feed / X / Shorts (`mobile-video.md`)
4. **AlternativeTo** — ~2026-08-05
5. **Warm intros** — ask 2–3 people who know you to try `/try` and reply with one sentence (creates quotes + real usage)

---

## 5. Kill / keep (2 weeks from now)

- Still **0** trials from all outbound + no `/try` traffic in Admin → cold email is not your channel; double down SEO + product + communities only.
- **Some** `/try` / sends but no replies → emails may be muted or spam; still convert in-product.
- **A reply that tries the product** → ask for quote; that one lead is worth more than 40 silent sends.
