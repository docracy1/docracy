# Backlink reclamation — social DM templates (X / LinkedIn / Facebook)

For the 23 personal contacts in `marketing/seo-research/docracy-backlink-contacts-personal-subset.csv`
only — these are named individuals with their own site, so a DM referencing their actual page feels
personal rather than a mail-merge blast. The other ~270 contacts are role-based company addresses
(support@, info@) with no individual to DM — email is still the right channel for those (see
`backlink-reclamation-email.md`).

Same pitch as the email version: their page links to docracy.com, which was acquired by eversign and
discontinued in 2019 — dead-link tip, not a cold pitch. Keep that framing regardless of platform.

## X (DM)

X DMs read best short and low-effort — one or two lines, no signature block.

```
Hey {{first_name}} — saw {{their_domain}} links to docracy.com, which got acquired and shut down back
in 2019 (redirects to a generic page now). Figured you'd want to know. No agenda, just a dead-link tip 🙂
```

If they're not a mutual/follower, a reply to a relevant old tweet (if they ever posted the link
themselves) often lands better than a cold DM — X gates DMs from non-followers on many accounts.

## LinkedIn

Two-step: a connection request note (300-character limit if they're not already a connection), then a
full message once connected.

**Connection request note (only if not yet connected):**
```
Hi {{first_name}} — noticed a dead link on {{their_domain}} to docracy.com (discontinued in 2019).
Wanted to flag it, no other agenda. Happy to connect either way.
```

**Full message (once connected, or if already 1st-degree):**
```
Hi {{first_name}},

Small thing — {{their_page_title}} on {{their_domain}} links to docracy.com, which was acquired by
eversign and shut down in 2019 (it just redirects to a generic page now, nothing relevant to what you
originally linked).

Not trying to sell anything, just flagging the dead link in case it's worth a quick fix.

Best,
{{your_name}}
```

## Facebook (Messenger)

Facebook DMs work best when there's a shared context (a mutual page, group, or their business Page) —
cold Messenger requests from strangers often land in the "Message requests" folder and go unread.
Best used for contacts with an active Facebook Page for their site/business, not personal profiles.

```
Hi {{first_name}}! Quick heads-up — {{their_domain}} has a link to docracy.com that's been dead since
2019 (the company got acquired and the domain just redirects now). Thought you'd want to know, no
strings attached.
```

## Notes on channel choice per contact

- Prefer LinkedIn for anyone whose site reads as professional/consulting (e.g., authors, consultants,
  personal-brand sites) — it's the platform where an unsolicited "your link is dead" note reads most
  normal.
- Use X only for contacts who are visibly active there (check their site for an X/Twitter link first —
  DMing someone who hasn't tweeted in years wastes the message).
- Facebook is the weakest fit for this list — most of the 23 are technical/developer personal sites,
  which skew toward X and LinkedIn, not Facebook. Only use it if their site explicitly links a Facebook
  Page.
- Email remains the fallback for anyone you can't find on any platform — most of the 23 already have a
  working email from the site's HTML, so social DMs are a supplement, not a replacement.
