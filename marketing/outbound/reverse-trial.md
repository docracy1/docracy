# Reverse trial — people who already touched Docracy

Warmest leads: they **sent** or **signed** a document. Don’t pitch like a stranger.

**When to use:** 1–3 days after they completed a send/sign (or when you see a reply to a transactional email).

CTA: `https://docracy.io/try` (senders) · `https://docracy.io/price` (if they hit limits) · account `https://docracy.io/login`

Sign-off:

```
Odo
Founder, Docracy
https://docracy.io
```

---

## How to find them

1. **Resend** — search recent `completion_*` / signing invites; reply in-thread if they answered.
2. **Admin → Signups / Growth** — new accounts that came from `ref=signer-completion`, `completion-email`, `status-*`.
3. **Your inbox** — anyone who emailed “what is this?” after getting a signing link.
4. ** preparer emails** you recognize from anonymous sends (if they opted into tips or replied).

Only email addresses that contacted you or used the product with you in the chain. Don’t cold-email random signers from other people’s docs unless they wrote first.
