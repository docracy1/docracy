### 3.3 Business activity and financial position

The chair reported on the year to date:

- 2025 has been a year of heavy investment in the rebuilt electronic-signature product and its go-to-market, mostly through external developers and service providers. Sales revenue recognised in the accounts is low (full-year 2025 sales revenue per the later annual report: 1 803 EUR, all Estonia; other operating income 46 EUR).
- Declared VAT-taxable turnover (KMD) was 48 565 EUR in Q1, 69 150 EUR in Q2 and 55 248 EUR in Q3 2025. These figures largely reflect reverse-charge services purchased from foreign suppliers declared in the VAT return and are not sales revenue.
- Operating expenses are running at roughly 55 000–70 000 EUR per quarter; the cumulative loss for January–September 2025 is approximately 180 000 EUR and the full-year loss is expected at about 220 000 EUR (actual result per the later annual report: −222 973 EUR).
- Operations are financed by short-term financing from the shareholder group; short-term liabilities are expected to reach approximately 214 000 EUR by year-end. The company has no bank loans, no tax arrears and no creditor claims; all tax returns have been filed.

**Resolution 3.** The board takes note of the report and approves the operating plan for Q4 2025, subject to the measures in resolutions 4 and 5.

### 3.4 Net assets below the statutory threshold – § 176 Commercial Code

Based on the interim figures, the company's net assets became negative during 2025 and are expected at approximately **−210 000 EUR** at 31.12.2025 (actual per the later annual report: −209 581 EUR). Net assets are therefore below half of the share capital and below the minimum share capital, which obliges the Management Board to convene the shareholders' meeting (§ 176 of the Commercial Code), which must decide on one of the following: a reduction or increase of share capital, other measures restoring net assets (e.g. shareholder contributions or conversion of shareholder loans into equity), dissolution, merger, division or transformation, or filing for bankruptcy.

**Resolution 4.** The board resolves to:
- (a) convene the sole shareholder RELACON OÜ without delay and submit the matter of the net assets for decision pursuant to § 176 of the Commercial Code; as the company has a single shareholder, the decision may be adopted as a written shareholder resolution without a meeting;
- (b) propose to the shareholder the following measures: (i) continuation of the company as a going concern, (ii) a written confirmation by RELACON OÜ that it will continue to fund operations and will not demand repayment of its receivables to the extent that this would endanger the company's solvency (subordination/letter of support), and (iii) restoration of net assets by converting shareholder receivables into equity (contribution to other equity or share capital increase) once the development phase is complete;
- (c) report the shareholder's decision in the next board minutes and in the management report of the annual report 2025.
Votes: for 1, against 0, abstained 0. Adopted.

### 3.5 Financing, cost control and going concern

**Resolution 5.** The board resolves to:
- (a) continue operations on a going-concern basis, relying on the shareholder's financing commitment;
- (b) document all financing from RELACON OÜ and other related parties in written loan/financing agreements at arm's length and disclose them as related-party transactions in the annual report;
- (c) review external development spending quarterly and prioritise revenue-generating features and paid plans so that the loss is reduced in 2026;
- (d) maintain timely filing of VAT returns and payment of all taxes so that the company keeps a clean tax record (currently no arrears).
Votes: for 1, against 0, abstained 0. Adopted.

### 3.6 Cash need for the next twelve months

The chair presented the liquidity position and the cash requirement until 30.09.2026:

- Free cash is minimal: current assets are expected at roughly 5 000 EUR at 31.12.2025 (actual per the later annual report: 4 637 EUR). All spending in 2025 has been funded by monthly drawdowns of short-term shareholder financing, which will stand at approximately 214 000 EUR at year-end.
- Q4 2025 spending is planned at about 45 000 EUR (declared VAT-taxable turnover Q4 2025, largely purchased services: 43 546 EUR). From Q1 2026 the external development programme is wound down and monthly spending is reduced to approximately **3 000–8 000 EUR**.
- The **cash need for Q4 2025 – Q3 2026 is approximately 60 000–70 000 EUR** (about 45 000 EUR in Q4 2025 and about 20 000 EUR in the first three quarters of 2026), before any revenue from paid plans. Revenue in this period is expected to cover only a small part of the costs.

**Resolution 6.** The board:
- (a) approves the cash plan for Q4 2025 – Q3 2026 with an estimated cash need of approximately 65 000 EUR;
- (b) resolves that the cash need is covered by the sole shareholder RELACON OÜ under the existing financing arrangement, increased by up to 70 000 EUR, and that the shareholder's written financing and subordination commitment (resolution 4(b)) shall explicitly cover this amount and period;
- (c) resolves to reduce the monthly cash burn from Q1 2026 to no more than 8 000 EUR by ending external development contracts, consolidating hosting and software subscriptions and keeping the board unpaid;
- (d) requires a monthly cash-flow report to the shareholder; any cost commitment above 5 000 EUR outside the cash plan requires prior consent of the shareholder.
Votes: for 1, against 0, abstained 0. Adopted.
