### 3.2 Change of business name and amended articles of association

The chair reported that the shareholder resolved to change the company's business name from **xosign OÜ** to **swipesign OÜ** and to adopt amended articles of association. The amended articles took effect and were registered in the Commercial Register on **16.08.2024**; the name xosign OÜ is now recorded as a former (invalid) name. The registered articles waive the notarial form requirement for the disposition of a share (transfer and pledge), which is permitted because the share capital of 10 000 EUR is fully paid in.

**Resolution 2.** The board confirms that the name change and the amended articles of association have been registered. The board resolves to:
- (a) use the business name swipesign OÜ on all invoices, contracts, the website, e-mail and other business documents from this date onward, and to re-issue any document templates still carrying the former name;
- (b) notify the bank, the Tax and Customs Board (VAT register data), service providers and customers of the new business name;
- (c) keep the product brand aligned with the new business name.
Votes: for 1, against 0, abstained 0. Adopted.

### 3.3 Transfer of the share to RELACON OÜ

On **20.08.2024** the sole share of the company (nominal value 10 000 EUR, 100 % of the share capital) was transferred to **RELACON OÜ** (registry code 16540477, Tallinn), a company wholly owned and represented by Reinhold Lackner. The Commercial Register shows RELACON OÜ as the sole shareholder from 20.08.2024. swipesign OÜ has thereby become a 100 % subsidiary of RELACON OÜ. The ultimate beneficial owner of swipesign OÜ remains Reinhold Lackner (registered beneficial owner since 18.01.2023; indirect ownership through RELACON OÜ and senior managing official).

**Resolution 3.** The board:
- (a) takes note of the share transfer and has updated the list of shareholders kept by the Management Board (§ 182 of the Commercial Code) to show RELACON OÜ as the holder of the sole share of 10 000 EUR as of 20.08.2024;
- (b) confirms that the shareholder data in the Commercial Register and the beneficial-owner data (Reinhold Lackner) are accurate and that no further beneficial-owner notification is required;
- (c) notes that transactions between swipesign OÜ and RELACON OÜ are related-party transactions and shall be documented at arm's length and disclosed in the annual report.
Votes: for 1, against 0, abstained 0. Adopted.

### 3.4 Business activity and financial position

The chair reported on the year to date:

- 2024 is the first year with sales revenue from the electronic-signature product. Declared VAT-taxable turnover grew each quarter (Q1 2024: 2 129 EUR; Q2 2024: 7 324 EUR; Q3 2024: approx. 12 500 EUR). Full-year sales revenue is expected to be approximately 19 000 EUR.
- Development spending exceeds revenue; a net loss of roughly 60 000 EUR is expected for 2024 (actual result per the later annual report: −59 565 EUR).
- To keep net assets positive while the product is being built, the shareholder has provided equity funding **without increasing the share capital** (contribution to other equity / voluntary reserve) of approximately 72 500 EUR. With this contribution the company's equity at year-end 2024 is expected at roughly 13 000 EUR (actual: 13 392 EUR) against total assets of about 26 000 EUR and short-term liabilities of about 12 600 EUR.
- The company has no employees, no bank loans, no tax arrears and no creditor claims.

**Resolution 4.** The board:
- (a) approves the continuation of product development and the 2024 operating plan;
- (b) accepts the shareholder's equity contribution without a share capital increase and resolves to record it in equity in the 2024 financial statements;
- (c) will continue to monitor net assets against § 176 of the Commercial Code and convene the shareholder immediately should net assets fall below half of the share capital.
Votes: for 1, against 0, abstained 0. Adopted.

### 3.5 Cash need for the next twelve months

The chair presented the liquidity position and the cash requirement until 30.09.2025:

- Free cash at year-end 2024 is expected at roughly 20 000–25 000 EUR (total current assets at 31.12.2024 per the later annual report: 26 008 EUR), after the shareholder's equity contribution received in 2024.
- The 2025 plan foresees a rebuild of the product and a go-to-market push carried out mainly by external developers and service providers, with monthly spending rising to **18 000–23 000 EUR** (approx. 55 000–70 000 EUR per quarter). Sales revenue during the rebuild is expected to stay low.
- The **cash need for Q4 2024 – Q3 2025 is approximately 200 000 EUR**, net of expected revenue and the opening cash balance. This cannot be covered from operations or from the share capital.

**Resolution 5.** The board:
- (a) approves the cash plan for Q4 2024 – Q3 2025 with an estimated cash need of approximately 200 000 EUR;
- (b) resolves to request from the sole shareholder RELACON OÜ a financing commitment of up to 220 000 EUR, to be drawn down in monthly tranches as short-term shareholder loans under a written framework agreement at arm's-length terms; the drawdowns are to be recorded as short-term liabilities and disclosed as related-party transactions;
- (c) notes that this financing will take net assets below the thresholds of § 176 of the Commercial Code in the course of 2025 unless part of it is provided as equity, and resolves to put the matter on the agenda of the next board meeting and to the shareholder in good time;
- (d) requires a monthly cash-flow report to the shareholder and a cash reserve covering at least one month of planned spending.
Votes: for 1, against 0, abstained 0. Adopted.
