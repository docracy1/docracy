## Verify before signing

- **Meeting dates and times** are placeholders in autumn of each year; adjust to the actual dates if meetings were held.
- **2024 equity contribution (~72 571 EUR)** is derived (equity 13 392 − share capital 10 000 − cumulative results −69 179) and should be checked against the equity note of the 2024 annual report.
- **Content of the articles amendments** of 16.08.2024, 12.03.2025 and 14.10.2026 is not visible in the public summary; the protocols only state that they were registered (and, for 16.08.2024, that the name changed).
- **Shareholder before 20.08.2024** is not visible without logging in to the register; the 2023 protocol therefore refers to "the founding shareholder".
- **§ 176 shareholder decision** (2025/2026) and the **financing/subordination commitment** by RELACON OÜ are drafted as proposals/decisions and must match the actual shareholder resolutions.
- **Explanation of the 2025 VAT-taxable turnover** (reverse-charge purchases) is an interpretation of the Tax Board data.
- **Quarterly loss figures** quoted for 2025 are Inforegister estimates, not accounting data.
